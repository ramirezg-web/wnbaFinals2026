WNBA Finals Series Forecaster

Turn this postseason's scores into team ratings and an exact best-of-7 forecast. Change the assumptions and watch the odds move.

1. Set up the series

Defaults are the 2026 Finals: the Valkyries host games 1, 2, 5 and 7, the Dream host games 3, 4 and 6. Update the series score as games finish.

Home-court advantage and noise are assumptions, not facts from the data. Try 0 and 5 to see how much the answer depends on them.

2. The forecast

How it works, and what it can't do

  1. Ratings. Each team gets a rating in points, fit from the margins of the 16 completed 2026 playoff games. A ridge penalty pulls ratings toward zero, because a team that played 5 games shouldn't be rated like a team that played 60.
  2. One game. Expected margin = rating gap ± home-court advantage. The chance of winning is a normal curve around that margin.
  3. The series. An exact calculation over every path to four wins in the 2-2-1-1-1 format, starting from the current score.

Limits

  • 16 games is a tiny sample. In a leave-one-game-out backtest the model did better than a coin flip on probability scoring, but with this few games that is a demonstration of method, not evidence the model is good.
  • It ignores injuries, rest, matchups, and who actually plays. A real forecast would add all of those.
  • Home teams won 12 of these 16 games, which makes the data lean toward a big home edge. The slider lets you override that.
